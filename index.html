NBP pension arrears estimator

Estimate what an NBP pensioner is owed under the Federal Constitutional Court's 16 September 2026 ruling in NBP vs. Khawaja Abdul Hameed Nasir (C.A. No. 1688/2021). That case is about periodical government pension increases NBP did not pass on after retirement — it is separate from the older 70%-vs-33% pension-factor case, which is already settled. This tool compares what the federal government announced each year against what the pensioner actually received, from the retirement date onward.
